Outfit-400.woff2 in this directory is a subset of the Outfit variable
font, served self-hosted; no CDN is called at runtime. Its weight axis
is bounded to 400 (Regular) through 500 (Medium) by the @font-face
declaration in src/styles/global.css, which is the only range the brand
uses. It is named for the lower bound because the website ships the same
bytes twice, once per weight, and this app keeps one copy.
